Issue: Account Locked

An account may be temporarily locked after five consecutive
failed login attempts.

Resolution:
Wait 15 minutes before attempting to log in again.
If the account remains locked, contact the support team.

Issue: Password Reset

Users can reset forgotten passwords by selecting
the Forgot Password option on the login page.

A password reset link will be sent to their registered
email address. The link expires after 15 minutes.

Issue: Application Crashing

If the application crashes during startup, restart it
and check whether the application is running the latest version.

If the issue persists, collect the error logs and contact
the technical support team.

Issue: Payment Failure

If a payment fails, check the payment status before
attempting another transaction.

If the amount has been deducted but the payment remains
unsuccessful, contact support with the transaction reference.

Issue: Account Recovery

Users who cannot access their registered email address
must contact customer support for identity verification
and account recovery assistance.
